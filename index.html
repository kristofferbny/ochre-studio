About

Ochre Studio design interiors in stone and wood, and make the objects that live within them. Our work begins with the material: where it comes from, how it feels underfoot or in the hand, and how it changes over decades of use. The result is rooms that feel calm and lived in, spaces that leave a lasting impression.

  1. 01 Consultation

    Every project begins with a conversation. We visit the space, study its light and proportions, and listen to how you want to live in it, before a single material is chosen.

  2. 02 Design & Concept

    We turn what we’ve learned into a clear direction: plans, sketches and material studies that show how the space will feel and work, refined with you until every room has a purpose.

  3. 03 Material Selection

    We source stone, wood and clay from quarries, mills and makers we know well. You’ll see and touch every sample, so each material is chosen for how it ages as much as how it looks.

  4. 04 Execution

    We oversee the build from first cut to final finish, working closely with trusted craftspeople. Being on site keeps every detail true to the design, from the joinery to the last stone laid.

  5. 05 Completion

    We hand over a finished space, often with objects from our studio made for it. Then we stay in touch, because natural materials keep changing, and we like to see how a room settles into life.

Objects Shaped Slowly In Clay

Alongside our interiors, we make a small collection of ceramics in our Oslo workshop. Each piece is thrown by hand, glazed in tones drawn from stone and earth, and made to be used every day rather than kept on a shelf.

Pieces are made in small editions and are not sold online. To ask about availability, commissions or tableware for a project, write to us at studio@ochre.com. Most pieces can be made to order within six to eight weeks.

Every space begins with listening.

Whether it’s a home, a hotel or a single room, we begin by understanding how a space is used, how light moves through it, and how it should feel to be in. The materials follow from there.

Let’s work together

Tell us about your project: where it is, what stage you’re at, and how you’d like the space to feel. We reply to every enquiry within two working days. For ceramics and editions, simply mention the piece you’re interested in.
