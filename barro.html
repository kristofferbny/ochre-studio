Barro

Earth, shaped and shown.

Barro is a gallery for ceramic art in Oaxaca. Clay and plaster carry the colour of the work into the rooms around it, creating a setting where the vessels feel at home. The architecture stays quiet, leaving space to look closely.

Rounded openings lead from one room to the next, while recessed shelves and simple plinths bring each piece into view. Warm light washes the display niches, picking out the irregular rims, handles and surfaces of the clay. Dark timber floors give the pale walls a grounded edge. Throughout, the materials are kept few, allowing small differences in texture and tone to hold the eye.
